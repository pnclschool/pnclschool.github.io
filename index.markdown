The Faculty of Philosophy at the University of Novi Sad (Serbia) is delighted to invite you to participate in the 7th International School in **Psycholinguistics, Neurolinguistics and Clinical Linguistics** to be held in person from 12 to 16 April 2027.

The PNCL School is designed for postgraduate and advanced undergraduate students interested in learning about the neurocognitive foundations of language and developing practical research skills, from translating research ideas into experimental designs to collecting and analysing data using experimental and statistical methods.

The intensive 40-hour programme offers two-hour sessions at two levels (introductory and advanced) across the three interrelated disciplines, taught by an international team of experts from Spain, France, the Netherlands, China, Slovenia and Serbia. For more information about the courses that the School offers, see [**the programme**](/programme/).

For information about how to apply, see [**the application page**](/apply/).

The School will be followed by the 14th edition of the Novi Sad Workshop in Psycholinguistic, Neurolinguistic and Clinical Linguistic Research (PNCLR 14), to be held on 17 April 2027. Participants in the School are encouraged to submit an abstract for a (poster) presentation at the workshop. For more information, visit [the website](https://www.ff.uns.ac.rs/sr/nauka/konferencije/konferencije-u-organizaciji-filozofskog-fakulteta/psycholinguistic-neurolinguistic-and-clinical-linguistic-research-pnclr).
