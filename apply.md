To apply for the School, please complete the [**Application Form**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScsbSCXfDclTeg9egX_PxjqziD6pMvUk-Jb_JjY2IlkIowwcQ/viewform?usp=publish-editor) by 15 January 2027, outlining your motivation for attending. Should the number of applications exceed the number of available places, priority will be given to graduate students with a demonstrated interest in the neurocognitive foundations of language. Applicants will be notified of the outcome of their application by the end of January 2027.

**Tuition fee: EUR 120**

The tuition fee must be received by the host institution by 1 March 2027, following notification of acceptance at the end of January. All bank transfer charges must be covered by the applicants.

Up to three students from the host institution are eligible for a discount. For further information, please contact the organisers at pncl@ff.uns.ac.rs.

The tuition fee covers participation in the School, course materials, coffee and tea during breaks, and admission as a non-presenting participant to the 14th Novi Sad Workshop in Psycholinguistic, Neurolinguistic and Clinical Linguistic Research (PNCLR 14), to be held at the Faculty of Philosophy on 17 April 2027.
