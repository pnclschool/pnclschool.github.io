The programme of the 7th PNCL School runs from 12 to 16 April 2027 and comprises 40 hours of instruction across three interrelated disciplines, with courses offered at introductory and advanced levels.

The daily schedule is provided below. Click on a course title to view its description and information about the instructor. Course materials, where applicable, will also be made available on the individual course pages.
