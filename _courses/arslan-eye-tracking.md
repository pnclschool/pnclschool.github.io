In this course, you will get the most important information about eye-tracking as a method used in language processing studies. Specifically, we will review 1) basic aspects of this technique, 2) the most commonly used eye-tracking measures, 3) different eye-tracking paradigms, e.g., the reading paradigm and the visual world paradigm, and 4) examples of studies with different populations. Newer trends in online experimental studies which combine multiple methods (e.g. eye-tracking and EEG) will also be mentioned.
