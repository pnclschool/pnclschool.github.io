This course will focus on speech and language disorders after brain damage in children. The first class (introductory) will familiarize students with acquired childhood aphasia across different clinical populations. The second class (advanced) will discuss the consequences of lesions to the cerebellum for speech and language, focusing on posterior fossa syndrome and the interplay between speech and language impairments in children with posterior fossa tumours.
