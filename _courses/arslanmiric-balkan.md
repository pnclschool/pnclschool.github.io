The linguistic diversity and complex contact situations in the Balkans present both opportunities and methodological challenges for experimental research. This course will examine how Balkan languages and varieties can be investigated using approaches from psycholinguistics, neurolinguistics, clinical linguistics, and language acquisition research. Based on a review of the existing literature and a survey of experts on Balkan languages, we will discuss the current state of research, identify understudied populations and linguistic phenomena, and examine barriers to experimental investigation, including limited linguistic resources, dialectal variation, and the lack of standardized assessment instruments. Particular attention will be given to methodological considerations in designing experimental studies and conducting fieldwork in multilingual and underrepresented language communities. The course will highlight how research on Balkan languages can contribute to the development and testing of broader theories of language processing, acquisition, and similar research areas.
