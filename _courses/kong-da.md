This course offers an in-depth exploration of discourse production in individuals with acquired neurogenic language disorders. Over the past two decades, significant advancements have been made in understanding the linguistic, cognitive, non-verbal, and social aspects of discourse deficits associated with conditions such as aphasia, traumatic brain injury, and dementia. Participants will gain an overview of common discourse challenges, explore current assessment strategies, and learn about innovative treatment approaches. Emphasizing emerging directions, the workshop will also highlight multi-modal frameworks for discourse rehabilitation and the use of big-data research to inform topic and vocabulary selection in language therapy, thereby enhancing evidence-based discourse analysis and intervention.
