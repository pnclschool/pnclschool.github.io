This course will present various theoretical approaches to the phenomenon of typical language acquisition, such as generative, cognitive and socio-cultural. The focus will be on the issues of innateness, universal grammar, poverty of stimulus argument, and critical period for language acquisition. Additionally, the course will present the milestones in language acquisition across different levels of linguistic structure.
