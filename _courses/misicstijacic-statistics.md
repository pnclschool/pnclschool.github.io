This course offers a gentle and accessible introduction to using mixed-effects regression models for psycho- and neurolinguistic data, by providing step-by-step tutorials in R studio. We will be using pieces of sample data from a reaction times experiment for the mixed-effect regression model, and from an eye-tracking experiment for a generalized mixed-effect regression model. The course requires no prerequisites; however, we recommend participants to bring along a laptop computer with R and R-studio installed.
